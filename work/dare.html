Case study · Mobile app

A social challenge app where every dare is earned

A client hired us to design a mobile app for iOS and Android where friends post dares, accept them, upload photo or video proof and earn points on a weekly leaderboard. We mapped the product, designed the screens and architected the back end.

The challenge

Make dares fun, fair and safe

Challenge apps live or die on trust. Proof has to be real, rewards can't be gamed, and risky content can't go public. The client needed a product plan that a dev team could build without guessing.

What we delivered
  • User flow & wireframes
    Seven core screens from onboarding to wallet, presented to the client as a clickable flow.
  • Full screen inventory
    53 mobile screens plus six admin views, with onboarding, upload, appeal and privacy states.
  • Requirements spec
    Numbered user stories, edge cases and acceptance tests for every launch feature.
  • Rewards that can't be gamed
    A server-side points ledger: points are in-app only and awarded exactly once per verified dare.
  • Safety by design
    Proof stays private until reviewed, with reporting, blocking and a moderation console.
  • Build plan
    Milestones from approved designs to store-ready builds, with testing and handoff gates.

How it works

From dare to reward in four steps

  1. Post a dare

    A friend or the community posts a challenge with a points reward.

  2. Accept

    Users accept or decline from the home feed.

  3. Prove it

    Upload a photo or video. It stays private until it's checked.

  4. Earn & rank

    Verified dares award points once and move you up the weekly leaderboard.
