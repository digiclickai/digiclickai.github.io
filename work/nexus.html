The challenge

Deals lived in WhatsApp threads and spreadsheets

Wholesalers retype stock into price lists, send quotes by hand and chase payments for weeks. Nothing connects, margins leak, and buyers can see more than they should.

What we built
  • Shareable price lists
    Turn this week’s stock into a private link buyers can open without an account; suppliers and costs stay hidden.
  • Quote → deal → invoice
    An accepted quote becomes a deal, then an invoice from the same record, with status tracked end to end.
  • Card payments to the business
    Invoices are paid by card through each business’s own Stripe account; the platform never holds customer money.
  • Plans & subscriptions
    Tiered monthly plans with a free trial, upgrades charged up front, and billing reminders.
  • AI assistant
    Ask questions about your own deals, stock and buyers in plain English.
  • Security by default
    Bot protection on sign-in, every workspace reviewed before it opens, private-by-default data and exportable records.
How it works

From first click to done, automatically

The parts a customer sees are backed by automations that run on a schedule and alert a human only when needed.

  1. Upload stock

    A business adds this week’s stock once.

  2. Share a price list

    Buyers get a private link with only their prices.

  3. Quote & confirm

    Quotes become deals with shipping and status tracked.

  4. Get paid

    The invoice goes out from the same record and is paid by card.
