The challenge

Thousands of parts, prices that move every day

Supplier costs, stock and photos change constantly. Keeping a wholesale catalog accurate by hand means wrong prices, out-of-stock orders and hours lost every week, while customers still expect fast answers and same-day shipping.

What we built
  • Supplier API sync
    A scheduled sync pulls cost, stock and product photos from the supplier API every few hours, with pricing floors so nothing sells below cost.
  • Conversion-focused storefront
    Shop-by-device navigation, compatibility and quality tier on every listing, and a dealer program with tiered wholesale pricing.
  • AI live chat
    A support assistant trained on store policies answers shipping, warranty and returns questions 24/7 and hands off to the support team when needed.
  • Branded transactional emails
    Order, shipping and account emails redesigned to match the brand instead of default templates.
  • Walmart Marketplace integration
    Listings, photos and daily order pulls connected through the Walmart API, with orders landing on the team task board.
  • Health monitoring
    Daily checks on the sync, listings and site, with alerts only when something needs a person.
How it works

From first click to done, automatically

The parts a customer sees are backed by automations that run on a schedule and alert a human only when needed.

  1. Supplier updates

    The supplier API is read on a schedule for new costs, stock and photos.

  2. Catalog adjusts

    Prices, stock and images update on the store automatically, within the pricing rules.

  3. Customers buy & ask

    Shoppers order 24/7; the AI assistant answers common questions instantly.

  4. Team handles exceptions

    Orders, marketplace sales and anything unusual land on the task board with alerts.
