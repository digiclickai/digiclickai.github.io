FAQ

Questions people ask

Straight answers before you reach out.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. Everything is built in your own accounts, with your logins, and documented. If we stop working together, it keeps running.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n, Zapier and Make for workflows; Notion, HubSpot and Google Workspace for data; WooCommerce, Shopify and Stripe for commerce; OpenAI and Claude for the AI steps; plus custom APIs and full custom builds when an app doesn't fit.

Can you build a whole website or platform, not just automations?

Yes. We build e-commerce stores, client portals, dashboards and full SaaS platforms, and we run two of our own. These are quoted after an audit.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so every project gets a custom quote after a free discovery call. As a guide, single workflows usually start around $1,200, full operations systems around $4,500, and websites or platforms are quoted on scope. You see the full price in writing before anything starts.

How fast can you start?

Audits are usually booked within a week. Builds start once the written scope is approved.

How do I pay?

You receive an invoice from DigiClick AI L.L.C. after approving the scope. Builds are 50% up front and 50% at sign-off; audits and Care Plans are paid up front. Bank transfer (ACH or international wire) is accepted.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in the US, the UAE and elsewhere, in English.

Is my data safe?

We use least-privilege access, never store your passwords, and remove our access at handoff unless you're on a Care Plan. We're happy to sign your NDA.
