How we work

Every project is scoped up front, so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs.

    A short call to understand your tools, your bottlenecks and what "done" looks like.

Questions people ask

Straight answers before you reach out.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. Everything is built in your own accounts, with your logins, and documented. If we stop working together, it keeps running.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n, Zapier and Make for workflows; Notion, Airtable, HubSpot and Google Workspace for data; WooCommerce, Shopify and Stripe for commerce; OpenAI and Claude for the AI steps; plus custom APIs when an app has no built-in connector.

How fast can you start?

Audits are usually booked within a week. Builds start once the written scope is approved.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% when you sign off against the acceptance checks. Care Plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled any month.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in the US, the UAE and elsewhere, in English.

Is my data safe?

We use least-privilege access, never store your passwords, and remove our access at handoff unless you're on a Care Plan. We're happy to sign your NDA.

Have a process that eats your week?

Tell us what you're doing by hand today. We take on a limited number of projects at a time and reply within two business days.
